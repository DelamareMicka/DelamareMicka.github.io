*Décider sans tout savoir* ("Deciding without knowing everything") is the first book in an upcoming collection, written for students before they join my research on pedagogical alignment. It introduces POMDPs (partially observable Markov decision processes), from Bayes' theorem to simulation, with tested Python examples and a running story to accompany the reading. The book is written in French.
