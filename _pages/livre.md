*An introduction to POMDPs, from Bayes' theorem to simulation*

- Plain-language intuition first, then rigor, chapter after chapter
- Every numerical example verified by a tested script
- numpy and matplotlib exercises to extend the reading
- Bibliography of 11 verified scientific references

The book is written in French.
